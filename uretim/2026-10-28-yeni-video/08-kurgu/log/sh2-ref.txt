In nineteen forty-six, U.S. debt was bigger than the whole economy. By nineteen seventy-four, it was under a quarter of it. And here's the strange part. The debt itself didn't shrink. It actually grew. The economy grew around it, and prices rose too. How? Thing one: it borrows in its own money. A family borrows dollars it can't make. This household borrows in dollars, and the dollar is its own currency. Thing two: it never retires. A family has to pay off its debts within one lifetime. A country has no last payday. A family can't grow its way out of a loan like that. A country did. This is history, not a forecast.
