Envelope four: defense. The entire military. Ships, planes, salaries, bases. Nine hundred sixty-five dollars. Now look at envelope five. No logo. No picture. Just one word: interest. About one thousand fifty dollars. That makes it the third-biggest bill on this table, after Social Security and Medicare. Read that again. This household pays its lenders more than it pays for its whole military. Interest alone eats about one in every five dollars that comes in the door. And it buys nothing. No road, no check, no ship. It's just the price of money borrowed in the past.
