How can a new rate change an old debt? Because the balance isn't one loan. It's many loans, and they keep coming due. Some last weeks. Some last decades. They don't come due all at once. They roll over a few at a time. Every time an old, cheap loan comes due, it's replaced with a more expensive one. In late October twenty twenty-six, new ten-year loans cost close to five percent. Even short-term borrowing cost about four percent.
