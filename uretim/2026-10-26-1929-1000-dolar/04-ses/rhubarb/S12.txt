Box Leo's thousand dollars never changes. Same stack of bills, every morning. But the world around the box does change. Prices are falling. Not just a few prices. The average price of what families buy. The official price index shows it. From September nineteen twenty-nine to July nineteen thirty-two, consumer prices fell by about twenty-one percent. So the same thousand dollars now buys what about one thousand two hundred seventy dollars bought in nineteen twenty-nine. Box Leo did nothing, and his money got stronger.
