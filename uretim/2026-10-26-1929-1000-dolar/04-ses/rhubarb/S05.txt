To see why, look at the last eight years. In August nineteen twenty-one, the Dow Jones Industrial Average stood at sixty-three. By September nineteen twenty-nine, it was at three hundred eighty-one. That's six times higher. Ordinary people, not just bankers, were putting their savings in. Many didn't even pay the full price. They put down part of it, sometimes as little as ten percent, and borrowed the rest. Stock Leo walks through the blue door on September third. That day, the Dow closes at three hundred eighty-one point one seven. It's the top. Nobody knows it yet.
