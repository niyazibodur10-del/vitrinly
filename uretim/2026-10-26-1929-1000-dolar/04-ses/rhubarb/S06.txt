For a few weeks, prices swing up and down, with sudden drops and quick recoveries. Then October arrives. On Monday, October twenty-eighth, the Dow falls nearly thirteen percent in a single day. On Tuesday, it falls almost twelve percent more. People will call them Black Monday and Black Tuesday. The borrowed money makes it worse. When prices drop, lenders want their loans covered, so people sell, and prices drop again. By mid-November, the Dow has lost almost half its value. Stock Leo's one thousand dollars is now about five hundred.
