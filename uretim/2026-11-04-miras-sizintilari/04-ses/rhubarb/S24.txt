A lot of this money reaches people in the middle of their lives, not at the start. And waiting has a quiet cost of its own. Even at the Fed's two percent inflation target, a dollar loses about a third of its buying power over twenty years. Money that arrives when you're starting out might help with a first home, or a first business. Money that arrives later often goes somewhere else. Your own retirement. Your own kids. Same boxes.
