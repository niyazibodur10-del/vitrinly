Stop two is care. As people get older, many need help with daily life. Getting dressed. Cooking. Taking medicine on time. U.S. government researchers estimate that more than half of Americans turning sixty-five will, at some point, need serious long-term care. About one in five will need it for more than five years. Sometimes that help comes from family, at home. Sometimes it means a nursing home. And that bill is big. In the U.S., a private room in a nursing home has a median cost of about one hundred thirty thousand dollars a year, says a twenty twenty-five survey by CareScout.
